# The Blameless Post-Mortem

An outage lasted 4 hours. Root cause: a deployment the junior pushed. But the real cause: no staging, no checklist, no tests, and a senior who approved the PR in 2 minutes while making coffee.

The room wants to blame the junior.

*Etsy's post-mortem culture: every incident is a learning opportunity. They've run over 1,000 post-mortems. Not one has resulted in someone being fired. But engineering quality has improved 10x. Blame is the enemy of learning.*
